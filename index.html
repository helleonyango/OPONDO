Our Story

Forged in Nairobi.
Built for the Relentless.

OPONDOFITNESS was born in a small garage gym in Nairobi — out of frustration. We were tired of flimsy imported gear that broke after a few heavy sessions and overpriced athletic wear that couldn't survive a real workout. We knew athletes in Kenya and across Africa deserved better.

So we built it ourselves. What started with a single power rack and a sewing machine has grown into a brand trusted by over 500 athletes. We source, test, and engineer every product — from our Power Racks and Olympic Barbells to our Performance Tees and Training Joggers — to withstand the hardest training you can throw at it.

We are not for those who want shortcuts. We are for the early risers, the last-rep pushers, the ones who earn every drop of sweat. Train Harder. Look Better. isn't just a slogan — it's a promise that every rep, every set, every session counts.
